Unravel is my most valued anime piece, one in which I would consider my most difficult anime piece. I started learning this piece with a friend simply due to my interest in the Anime TV Show: Tokyo Ghoul. Additionally, Animenz, being the most famous anime adapter/composer, creates some of the most beautiful yet difficult pieces to play and I love to challenge myself with them!
