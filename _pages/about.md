I am an incoming second-year at the University of British Columbia, studying Data Science and specializing in Artificial Intelligence (AI). 

Before joining UBC, I participated in the AI Scholars program and the 1:1 AI Research Mentorship at Inspirit AI. I also participated in a game development summer program held at DigiPen Institute of Technology. Since then, I have been cultivating my enthusiasm for AI through the UBC Aerospace Club (ARRC) and independent projects.

My current interests lie in integrating AI into robotics as well as optimizing such automated control systems. This summer, I am the leader and founder of a research group, supervised by Dr. Hakat Tai, postdoctorate researcher working under Dr. Yusuf Altintas, in which we explore control systems through vision-driven control. In collaboration with Dr. Altintas' lab, the Mechanical Automation Laboratory (MAL) at UBC, we are pursuing a novel Reinforcement Learning (RL) model as the agent that controls our robotic arm.

Please visit our github organization [Your Remote Arm](https://github.com/Your-Remote-Arm) to learn about our up-to-date research projects.
