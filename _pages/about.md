I am an incoming second-year Computer Science student at the University of British Columbia, specializing in Artificial Intelligence. 

Before UBC, I gained early exposure to AI through the Inspirit AI Scholars program and its 1:1 Research Mentorship, as well as a game development summer program at the DigiPen Institute of Technology. Since then, I have continued to cultivate my passion for computer science through my involvement in Engineering Design Teams and independent projects.

My current interests lie in Software Development Engineering and Robotics. Over the past year, I've had lots of fun designing AI models for my robotics team, developing fullstack applications in hackathons, and even assisting my mentor in his research on CNC machines. I created a cool AI gym assistant for a hackathon in which I won multiple awards such as third-place, best solo hackathon, best first-time hackathon. I also implemented amazing video models that helped the UBC's UAV team reach second-place in the Aerial Evolution Association Competition in Canada. I've also helped my mentor design a reinforcement learning model which is being used for spindle health detection in CNC machines. This year, I've joined the UBC Rover team as part of the software sub-team and am responsible for seeing through the URDF modeling, robot navigation, RL autononmy, and overall robotic system in preparation for the Canadian International Rover Challenge (CIRC) and the University Rover Challenge (URC) in 2026.

Separately, I like to consider myself a bit nerdy but at the same time, athletic and possibly a happy blabbermouth. I love to meddle with operating systems and have been actively breaking my OS since starting my Arch Linux journey last year. I also pride myself in always "being happy," even when my friends ragebait me. Also, I'm very passionate about tennis and try to play 2-3 times a week. I also enjoy playing piano for my friends and try to practice at least 30 minutes every day. I’m also big on just hanging out with my friends, exploring new study spots, and finding excuses to enjoy some fresh air (against all stereotypes).

Actively looking for internship summer 2026 positions in Software Engineering and Robotics.
